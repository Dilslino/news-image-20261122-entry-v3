A one-page newspaper front page summing up the world's news of
**Sunday, 22 November 2026** — 42 stories across 9 regions, led by the
collapse of the COP31 climate talks in Antalya, Turkey.

## The image

`entry.png` (1024 × 1024) is a broadsheet front page with a new
three-column architecture: a masthead naming the day, a lead story on
COP31, and 42 numbered briefs grouped under nine section headers —
North America, Latin America, Europe, Middle East, Africa, South Asia,
East Asia, Southeast Asia & Oceania, and Science & Sport. Every story
is a real event that happened, or was first reported, on 22 November
2026, checked against same-day reporting (AP, Reuters, AFP, UN, ICJ
and wire coverage).

The Khartoum drone strikes and Sudan's claim that the drones were launched
from Ethiopia are carried as two separate briefs, and country names are
spelled in full.

## How it runs

`main.py` is a standard-library-only copy script: it copies `entry.png`
into the directory named by `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default `/output`).
It uses no network and no third-party packages, and running it twice
produces byte-identical output.
